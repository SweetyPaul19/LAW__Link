Know Your Rights: Dowry Prohibition

  • The Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961 makes giving, taking, or demanding dowry a punishable offence.
  • Section 498A, IPC (now Section 85, Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita) covers cruelty by husband or his relatives, including dowry harassment.
  • You can file a complaint at the nearest police station, a Women's Cell, or approach a Dowry Prohibition Officer.
  • Keep evidence: messages, receipts, witness names, and dated notes of every incident — SafeBond can help you log these privately.

Know Your Rights: Domestic Violence

  • The Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 (PWDVA) covers physical, emotional, sexual, verbal, and economic abuse.
  • You can seek a Protection Order, Residence Order, and Monetary Relief through a Magistrate.
  • A Protection Officer or registered NGO can help you file a Domestic Incident Report (DIR) free of cost.
  • You do not need to file for divorce to seek protection — DV relief is available regardless of your marital proceedings.

Know Your Rights: Divorce

  • Grounds for divorce vary by personal law (e.g. Hindu Marriage Act, Special Marriage Act, Muslim Personal Law) and typically include cruelty, desertion, and mutual consent.
  • Mutual consent divorce is usually faster and requires both parties to agree on terms including maintenance and custody.
  • You may be entitled to maintenance/alimony and child custody arrangements — courts prioritise the child's welfare.
  • Consider mediation or a family court counsellor before filing, where available — it can reduce cost and time.
